# Spacecraft artwork

`Assets/IonFrontier/Resources/ion-fleet.png` was generated with the built-in image_gen tool for this game. The complete original PNG, including its alpha channel, is preserved. Sprite regions are selected by `IonArt.cs`; the raster image was not manually repainted.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one production sprite atlas for a Unity 2D vertical spaceship shooter. Create ONE square 2048x2048 transparent PNG atlas with a strict evenly spaced 2x2 grid of FOUR separate spacecraft, each centered inside its own 1024x1024 quadrant, with at least 130px transparent margin around each. True orthographic TOP DOWN view (not isometric), sharp symmetrical silhouettes, hand-painted premium arcade sci-fi metal, clean readable at 80 pixels on screen, detailed but not noisy. Top-left: heroic sleek silver/ivory and dark navy player interceptor, triangular swept wings, cyan cockpit and twin cyan engine details, NOSE POINTS STRAIGHT UP. Top-right: compact angular hostile drone fighter, dark steel body with vermilion red illuminated panels, sharp wings, NOSE POINTS STRAIGHT DOWN. Bottom-left: hostile advanced interceptor, distinctive forked wings, dark gunmetal with orange/amber energy, NOSE POINTS STRAIGHT DOWN. Bottom-right: large imposing enemy command ship with broad armored wings and center core, dark titanium and burnt orange accents, NOSE POINTS STRAIGHT DOWN. All four have consistent art direction and lighting, realistic material highlights with crisp restrained glow. No perspective tilt. No cast shadow outside the spacecraft. No labels, NO TEXT, no logo, no UI, no stars, no scene, no trails, no bullets, no birds, no chicken or eggs. Only the four spacecraft on actual transparent background.

## Other assets

Starfield, distant planet, plasma bolts, trails, rings and impact textures are generated procedurally by the game. Laser, impact, explosion, pickup, dash and ambient music clips are synthesized by `IonArt.cs` at startup.

The Vietnamese interface uses the Liberation Sans font bundled as Resources/Interface.ttf. Its original SIL Open Font License is included beside it as Interface-OFL.txt. This clean project includes no external sound recordings or legacy game artwork.
